《蛊真人·青茅山》素材来源 / Asset credits

1. Fang Yuan (方源) walk sheet (16x16 x 4x4) and bust portrait (48x48)
   Original pixel art created for this game (long jet-black hair, pale skin, half-lidded dark eyes, white scarf, pale-grey cloak over charcoal robe).

2. Ninja Adventure (Superpowers asset pack) by Pixel-boy (Sparklin Labs)
   Source: https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs/tree/master/ninja-adventure
   Also: https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack
   License: CC0 1.0 Universal (attribution not required, given anyway)
   Used: characters/25 (方正, scarf recolored blue), characters/9 (学堂家老), 10 (族长古月博), 13 (舅舅古月冻土),
         5 (舅母), 14 (贾家商队), 2 (古月漠北), 6 (古月赤城), matching faceset/*.png portraits,
         background-elements/tileset.png (4 houses).

3. Ninja Adventure Godot project by pixel-boy
   Source: https://github.com/pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure (content/character/pig/pig.png)
   License: CC0 1.0
   Used: pig sprite, recolored dark brown as 山野猪.

4. "Wolf Pack! 32x32 Walking Wolf Animation" by patvanmackelberg
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/wolf-pack-32x32-walking-wolf-animation (Wolfpack.png)
   License: CC0
   Used: gray wolf -> blue electric 电狼; white wolf -> pale 豪电狼; black wolf -> indigo 雷冠头狼 with added lightning crown.

Terrain tiles (grass, bamboo, rock, cave, path, palisade) are original pixel art drawn in code.

5. Prologue and Qing Mao chapter sprites, drawn for this game
   Older Fang Yuan (hero_old, face_hero_old), righteous-path vanguard and alliance leader,
   Bai Ningbing (银发白衣的年轻男子，容貌男女莫辨；青绿色领口与腰带，见 tools/draw_bnb.py), Xiong-clan fighter, blood bat, frost wolf, armored bear,
   and the first-generation portrait. See tools/draw_prologue.py and tools/draw_ch23.py.
   These are original pixel drawings, not third-party assets.

The title banner is assembled from the same atlas by tools/rebuild_all.py.
It is still the original low-resolution pixel painting.

6. In-game “类像素” layer (guzhenren/hd.js)
   Map tiles, houses, and the figures used on the map and in battle are drawn
   at higher detail with a limited palette, soft gradients, highlights, and fog.
   Fang Yuan and Bai Ningbing keep the same costumes. The title banner, stone
   steles, and the smaller NPC dialogue portraits still use the older atlas art.
